01 Mission
The Problem Creative industry support

Meaningful mentorship in the creative industries has long been reserved for a select few. We're changing that.

What We Do Our approach

Prestigious programs like major arts fellowships and fashion funds serve an important purpose - but they reach only a handful of people each year. The vast majority of working creatives face real, pressing questions about their practice and their business with no clear path to expert help.

We fill that gap. Through a simple submission process, we match independent creatives with experienced legal and financial professionals - for direct, one-on-one consultations, fully covered by the Fund.

Who We Serve Our community

Independent creatives at any stage of their practice or business -- artists, designers, photographers, musicians, and emerging brand founders. Particularly those who have never had access to the kind of legal and financial guidance that larger organizations take for granted.

02 How It Works
01
Submit Your Question

Tell us about your practice and the specific challenge or opportunity you're navigating. No pitch deck required - just clarity about what you need help with.

02
We Review & Match

Our team reviews your submission and identifies an expert whose experience directly applies to your question. This typically takes 5-7 business days.

03
The Consultation

A 1-2 hour session, fully covered by the Fund. Come with questions, documents, or a specific problem to work through. The time is yours.

04
What Comes Next

If you and your expert want to continue working together, we make that introduction - and connect you to additional resources, referrals, and relevant programming.

01
Join the Network

Experts apply to join our advisory network. We welcome attorneys, accountants, CFOs, and financial advisors with experience in the creative industries.

02
Receive a Match

When a submission aligns with your expertise, we'll send a brief summary of the situation. You decide whether to participate - there's no obligation on any given request.

03
Contribute Your Time

Partners donate their time or participate for a fee paid for by the Fund -- whichever works best for them. Sessions are 1-2 hours, scheduled at your convenience, remotely or in person.

04
Build the Community

Expert partners are recognized across our communications and events. Many find these engagements lead to meaningful professional relationships and ongoing community ties.
