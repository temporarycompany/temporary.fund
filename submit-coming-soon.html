- What to expect at launch
Submit a request For creatives

Describe your practice and the specific legal or financial challenge you are navigating. Our team will review your submission and match you with the right expert. No competitive application, no fee, no gatekeeping.

The consultation What happens next

A 1-2 hour session with an expert partner, fully covered by the Fund. If you and your expert want to continue working together, we make that introduction. Our role ends at the initial consultation.

Who qualifies Eligibility

Independent creatives and founders of independently operated creative businesses based in the United States, who do not have legal or financial counsel on staff or on retainer. Full eligibility criteria
